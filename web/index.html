N° 03 — Fairness

Neither side holds the dice

  1. Before any customer exists a secret is hashed a hundred thousand times and the last link is published on-chain.
  2. When you open a box your browser adds a seed of its own. We cannot know it in advance, and we cannot change what we already published.
  3. When the draw settles the next link is revealed. The contract accepts only the true one.
  4. The result comes from both seeds, your address and the draw number. Every revealed seed is public, so anyone can recompute any draw.

If a draw is ever not settled within ten minutes, you take your HYPE back from the contract yourself. That path does not need us to be awake, willing or alive.
