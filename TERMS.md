## 3. What Amphora is — and what it is not

**It is:** a game of chance with published odds, in which each box yields exactly
one graded, numbered certificate recording ownership of a fragment of a real
artwork held by the studio.

**It is not:**

- an investment, a fund, or a security;
- a promise that anything you buy will hold or gain value;
- a means of storing value;
- regulated financial advice of any kind.

**Expected value is below the box price and we publish it.** At the time of
writing, a box has an expected value of 74% of its price (0.37 HYPE on a 0.5 HYPE box). The difference funds the
studio, the vault, and the physical programme. Buy boxes because you want the
objects, not because you expect a return.

## 5. Buying a box

- Boxes are priced and paid in HYPE, the native token of the Elysium network. The price is shown before you buy.
- **HYPE is a volatile cryptoasset, not a stablecoin.** The value of a box, of a mark, and of anything the desk pays you will rise and fall against your own currency. Marks and desk prices are fixed in HYPE, not in dollars.
- Payment is made by blockchain transaction. Network fees are yours.
- **A purchase is final once the transaction confirms.** There is no cooling-off
  period, because the outcome is generated immediately and cannot be un-generated.
  This is a permitted exception under consumer contract rules for goods made to
  order and for gaming outcomes.
- If a draw is not settled within ten minutes, you may reclaim the full purchase
  price from the contract yourself. That mechanism is on-chain and does not
  require us.

## 6. Odds and fairness

We publish the exact probability of every class before you open anything, and the
probabilities live on-chain.

Outcomes are produced by commit-reveal: we commit a chain of secret seeds on-chain
before any draw exists; your browser contributes a seed we cannot know in advance;
the result is derived from both. We cannot choose your outcome, and you can verify
any draw yourself. The method is documented at /docs.

We may change the published odds for future draws. Any change takes effect on-chain
and is visible before it applies. **We cannot change the odds of a draw already
made.**

## 7. What a certificate is

A certificate is a non-fungible token in your wallet recording: the work, your
edition number, your share count, the value at mint, and the draw that produced it.

Holding a certificate gives you:

- the recorded claim to that fragment of that work, within its stated edition;
- the right to sell it back to the desk on the published terms;
- the right, when redemption opens, to claim the corresponding physical card.

Holding a certificate does **not** give you: title to the underlying physical
artwork, any right to possess it, any share of studio revenue, any voting right,
or any entitlement to dividends or distributions.

---

## 8. The desk

The desk is a standing offer to buy any unredeemed certificate at **80% of its
published mark**, paid immediately.

- The mark is our published valuation. We set it, we publish it, and we change it
  only by publishing the change.
- The desk price follows the mark. If the mark falls, the desk price falls.
- We may suspend the desk if we are unable to operate it safely — for example
  during a security incident. Any suspension will be announced, with a reason.
- The desk is not a market. It is a floor we choose to provide, and while it is
  open we honour it.

## 9. Redemption and physical items

When redemption opens, you may claim a physical card for a certificate you hold.

- **Redemption is permanent.** On claiming, the certificate locks to the physical
  card and can no longer be sold to the desk or transferred separately. One number,
  one card, for good.
- Physical items are produced to order. Delivery times and shipping costs are shown
  before you confirm.
- Because each card is made to your specific edition number, **redemption is not
  cancellable** once production begins.
- Damaged or lost-in-transit items are replaced where the carrier confirms the
  loss. Contact us within 30 days.
- Physical cards contain an NFC chip that links to the certificate's page. We do
  not guarantee that link will work with every device forever.

## 13. Risk

- **This is chance.** Most boxes yield common fragments. You may spend money and
  receive nothing you value.
- **Nothing is guaranteed to hold value.** Marks are our valuations, not market
  prices.
- **Blockchain transactions are irreversible.** Mistakes cannot be undone.
- **Smart contracts can contain flaws.** Ours are tested and their source is
  published; that reduces risk, it does not remove it.
- **Preview periods use test networks and no real funds.** Nothing acquired on a
  test network has value or carries over.

Only spend what you would be comfortable losing. If gambling is causing you harm,
stop, and see our responsible play page for support organisations.
